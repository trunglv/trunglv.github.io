### Overview

ShippingPaymentFilter lets you control which shipping and payment methods are shown at checkout in Magento 1, so you can tailor available options to the order, customer, or other conditions.

### Support

Free and open-source. See the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/trunglv/ShippingPaymentFilter) for installation and usage details.
