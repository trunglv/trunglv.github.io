### Overview

Private Store restricts your Magento 2 storefront so that only logged-in customers can browse it. Visitors who aren't signed in are redirected to the login page instead of seeing your catalog.

This is handy for B2B or B2E (business-to-employee) stores where pricing and products should stay private to approved accounts.

### Key features

- Blocks guest access to the storefront, redirecting to login/registration
- Configurable per store view
- Allowlist specific pages (e.g. CMS pages) that should remain public
