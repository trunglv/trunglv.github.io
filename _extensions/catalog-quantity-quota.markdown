### Overview

Catalog Quantity Quota lets you cap how many units of a product (or category) a customer or customer group can purchase, over a given period. Useful for B2B stores managing limited stock, fairness across resellers, or contractual purchase limits.

### Key features

- Set quotas per customer group or per individual customer
- Apply quotas per product or per category
- Quota windows (e.g. per order, per month)
